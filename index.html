Introduction

The keywords to this site in the <meta> tag were chosen to include what the page is about, why it was made, and some other related words from the heading elements and the nav bar. The words signal that the site is about the mobile game Pokémon GO, which is my personal favorite hobby, and that it is the final project for the class CGS 1820, Web Authoring, at Santa Fe College. The description was created based on the same reasons as the keywords, just in a sentence format. The <h1> and <h2> heading elements were created to reflect what the main page content would be about and what the main section content would be covering, respectively. The kewords, description, heading elements, and body text all work together to theoretically gain a good relevance score, since those elements being on the site and having them correlated to each other are the steps of SEO.
